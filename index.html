What we handle

One team takes your cargo from the port of loading to the receiver in Syria, so you have a single contact for freight, paperwork and delivery.

Ask about your shipment

  • Sea and land freight

    Full and shared container loads (FCL and LCL) to Latakia and Tartus, with overland trucking when time matters.

  • Customs clearance

    Tariff classification, duty calculation, import permits and the full document set, prepared before your cargo arrives.

  • Cargo protection

  • Delivery across Syria

    Port-to-door trucking to Damascus, Aleppo, Homs and other cities, with an update at every stage.
